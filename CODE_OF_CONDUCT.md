# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all participants in the Inventory CLI (`inv`) project, regardless of background, identity, experience, or skill level.

## Standards of Behavior

We expect community participants to:
- Be respectful, constructive, and collaborative in discussions and issue reports.
- Focus on technical merit, objective evidence, and the product's safety invariants.
- Gracefully accept constructive feedback and differing technical approaches.
- Respect the time and contributions of fellow maintainers and users.

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, abusive language, discriminatory commentary, and disruptive behavior in project forums.

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for maintaining the health and constructive tone of the project repository. Issues, comments, and pull requests that violate these standards will be moderated, edited, or removed.

## Reporting Inquiries

To report concerns or violations of this conduct standard, please contact the project maintainer at `vinit@example.com`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality.
